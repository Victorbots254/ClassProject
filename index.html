Task 1 & 2 Heritage

Rooted in Craft, Time & Honest Baking

What does “LR” stand for? It represents Loaf & Rye, and its creator, Loren.

During our initial brand research, we took inspiration from three legendary artisan bakeries: Daily Bread (Auckland) for its natural warmth and local ingredients, Tartine (San Francisco) for simple and confident craft communication, and Levain (New York) for its iconic cookie gift boxes.

“LR Bakery provides a clean and versatile foundation. The serif initials convey timeless tradition, while the wheat stalk and loaf curves celebrate natural baking.”

Every loaf undergoes a 36-hour slow fermentation cycle, allowing wild yeast and lactobacilli to break down gluten and unlock deep caramelized wheat aromas.

Approved Final Logo (Task 2)
  • Brand Palette: Cocoa brown, caramel, kraft, and cream.
  • Typography: Classic Serif (Playfair Display) + Clean Sans-Serif (Plus Jakarta Sans).

Dieline & Structural Architecture

Packaging Specifications

Refined from preliminary sketches to achieve optimal product visibility, food protection, and stackable strength.

  • Material Stock A2 Cardboard (280–350 gsm Kraft)
  • Display Window Food-safe clear acetate film
  • Capacity 6 Handcrafted Cookies (Net Wt 240g)
  • Color Palette Kraft board, Cream interior, Cocoa print
  • Closure Style Tuck-in closing flaps with safety seal tab
  • Sustainability 100% Biodegradable board & soy-based inks

Standard 7-Step Assembly Workflow

1

Print

Print packaging template on A2 cardboard (280–350 gsm) at 100% scale.

2

Cut

Carefully cut along solid lines, including the transparent window opening.

3

Score

Lightly score dashed fold lines using a ruler and blunt scoring tool.

4

Window

Attach clear food-safe acetate film behind the window opening with adhesive.

5

Fold

Fold along scored lines to form front, back, sides, and closing flaps.

6

Glue

Apply adhesive to the designated glue tab and secure to side panel.

7

Assemble

Lock bottom flaps and shape into its final rectangular gift box.
